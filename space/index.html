Private by design

PaddleOCR, EasyOCR, Tesseract, GLM-OCR, LightOnOCR and the PDF text layer read your page inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored. The Runemic engine is the one exception: it sends the page to api.runemic.com, which reads it and doesn't keep it.

Reading thousands of pages, or Persian, Urdu and old scans that need to be right? Runemic's API reads them in one call, with a free credit to start.

OCR Arena is made by Runemic. Each engine keeps its own licence; follow it when you use the output. Models: PaddlePaddle and EasyOCR (Jaided AI) on Hugging Face, Tesseract.js, pdf.js.
